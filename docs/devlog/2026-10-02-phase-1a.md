## Decisions and open issues
- **scoring_model = `claude-opus-5-5` at $4 / $20 per MTok** (prices checked 2026-10-02).
  Owner's choice, made for pick quality (criterion 9). I first committed `claude-sonnet-5-5`
  ($2 / $10) and switched after review.
- **Known conflict with criterion 12.** With the plan's estimate of about 60k input and
  18k output tokens per hour of video, Opus 5.5 costs about $0.24 + $0.36 = $0.60 per hour,
  over the $0.50 target. Sonnet 5.5 would cost about $0.30. Opus 5.5 can't turn thinking
  off, and thinking tokens bill as output, so the real cost may be higher. Phase 4 will
  measure real token counts, then either:
  - tune the prompt, `batch_size` and effort (Opus 5.5 defaults to `medium`; set it
    explicitly) to get under $0.50, or
  - raise the criterion 12 target in the plan and record why here.
- **Flag for phase 4:** the plan says to score "at temperature 0", but current models
  reject non-default sampling parameters (Opus 5.5 returns 400 for `temperature`).
  Phase 4 must get repeatability from the cache plus a fixed prompt and structured output,
  and the plan or ADR 0003 should be updated to match.
- `whisper_engine = "faster-whisper"`, `whisper_model = "small.en"`: placeholders until
  phase 2 measures both engines (ADR 0002).
- Weights (0.30 / 0.25 / 0.20 / 0.10 / 0.10 / 0.05) are untuned starting values for phase 9.
